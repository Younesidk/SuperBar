Four directions for the SuperBar app icon

Every preview on this page is rendered from the same SVG you download, at real pixel sizes. Use the Stage switch to flip the large previews and the Control Center mockup between Windows dark and light.

Icons are drawn on a 256 × 256 canvas with a 16 px safe margin (20 px on most edges). Layer shadows are clipped to the shapes they fall on, so nothing bleeds onto the taskbar. Neighbouring taskbar icons in the mockups are generic placeholders.
